# The Prince Who Kept Showing Up — Series Plan

A ten-story arc featuring Prince Spencer. Planned 2026-09-15. The first drafts were removed for quality reasons; this doc preserves the plan so the series can be regenerated.

Original outlines and drafts are in git history at commit `9a26573` if a beat needs recovering.

## The frame

Spencer wants to tell Savannah how he feels. Before he does, he seeks out the wise figures and critters of the kingdom for guidance and approval. Each story he learns one thing and ends one step closer. The last story is the confession.

**Savannah is still the hero.** Spencer is the seeker, but every story's problem is solved by Savannah, sometimes without knowing the quest is about her. His growth is the heart; her courage is the climax. He never rescues her.

**Shared opener:** "Prince Spencer had a secret, and the whole kingdom knew it but one."

**Running thread:** the kingdom slowly conspires to help him, and by the end everyone but Savannah has guessed.

## The ten stories, in order

| # | Title | Guide(s) | Idea |
|---|-------|----------|------|
| 1 | Big Al's Only Question | Big Al, Finnegan, Thistle, Ollie | Spencer expects a grand test. Big Al asks one thing: "What makes her laugh?" Spencer spends the story finding out. Savannah ends up laughing at Al's old joke, not Spencer's. |
| 2 | The Cup of Exactly Right | Wilma, Hazel | Wilma teaches him Savannah's favorite drink. Every batch is wrong until he stops rushing and listens. Wilma already knows why he's asking. |
| 3 | Luna's Approval | Luna, Cora, Rascal | The hardest judge. Luna sits on him, out-barks him, steals his snacks. He wins by loving her exactly as much as Savannah does, which Luna finds suspicious and then wonderful. |
| 4 | The Great Makeover | Cora, Finnegan, Pepper | The critters give him a "royal" makeover: shiny bits, fox-styled hair, skunk-approved cologne. Savannah likes him best once it's all washed off. A critter nearly spills the secret. |
| 5 | The Bench That Took All Winter | Big Al, Mossy, Digby | Woodworking goes badly. Twist: Al never expected him to build it alone. Savannah and Spencer finish it together, and it's their bench, not his gift to her. |
| 6 | The Letter That Wouldn't Hold Still | Wilma, Pip, Thistle | He writes a love letter and the words hop off the page and hide in the forest. The critters chase them down. It reassembles as three words. |
| 7 | The Song for Savannah | Cora, Clover, music faire folk | He wants to write her a song but can't sing. Each critter carries one line. Savannah hums along without knowing it's about her. |
| 8 | The Map of Favorite Places | Bramble, Digby | They chart every spot she loves so he can pick the right one. The map keeps growing because she keeps finding new favorites. The place doesn't matter. |
| 9 | The Night Spencer Got Lost | Fireflies, Ollie, Clover | He sets out by moonlight to say it and gets turned around. Savannah, out on her own adventure, finds him. |
| 10 | The Prince Who Asked the Eldest Tree | The Eldest Tree, Luna | Last stop, thousand-year wisdom. The tree says: she already knows, go home. He does, and says it, on the bench, with Luna sitting on both of them. |

Stories 9 and 10 can merge if ten feels long.

## Regeneration notes

- Spencer is a generic fairy-tale prince: kind, strong, brave, learning to be worthy of Savannah. See `characters.md`.
- Real-life details (favorite drinks, instruments, spelling, home projects) are seeds for one beat in one story at most. They are not character traits and must not recur across the series.
- Follow the comparison rule in `AGENTS.md`. The removed drafts failed because every element got stacked "it was X like Y" descriptions, most of them meaningless.
- Avoid roll-call sequences (one line per villager, one line per critter saying yes). Three is the limit.
