# Outline: The Great Makeover of Prince Spencer

**Series:** The Prince Who Kept Showing Up, 4 of 10
**Premise:** Cora, Finnegan, and Pepper decide a prince needs to look the part before he can say what he means, and give Spencer a "royal" makeover: crow-collected shiny bits, fox-styled hair, and a skunk-approved cologne. On the day of the Ribbon Festival, the maypole ribbons are tangled beyond untangling, and it takes Savannah's puzzle-mind, Spencer's strength, and one very shiny, very jangly prince to fix it. She likes him best once it is all washed off.
**Characters:** Princess Savannah, Prince Spencer, Luna, Cora the Crow, Finnegan the Fox, Pepper the Skunk, Thistle the Rabbit
**Setting:** The village green on Ribbon Festival morning, with the tall maypole in the middle and a hundred colored ribbons that are supposed to hang straight. Finnegan's den, redecorated for the day as a salon.

## Beats

1. **The peaceful kingdom.** Ribbon Festival morning. Prince Spencer had a secret, and the whole kingdom knew it but one, and three members of the kingdom had decided to help whether he liked it or not. Cora has been collecting for weeks. Finnegan has a comb. Pepper has been practicing a cologne from wild mint and creek water and is very, very careful about it.

2. **The problem.** The makeover. Cora pins on every shiny thing she owns: buttons, a spoon, a bit of mirror, a bell. Finnegan styles his hair up and forward "like a fox in a good mood." Pepper applies the cologne with great ceremony, and everyone agrees it is lovely and also that you can smell it from the next meadow. Spencer looks in the mirror-bit and sees someone he does not recognize. He jangles when he walks. Thistle says he looks like a wind chime that got dressed up.

3. **The call.** Down on the green, the festival cannot start: overnight the wind wound all one hundred ribbons around the maypole into one enormous knot, and every grown-up who has pulled at it has made it worse. Savannah, who loves nothing more than a puzzle, walks around it three times and says, "Nobody pull anything. I need to see it." She needs someone strong to hold the pole steady while she works, because it is leaning. Guess who jangles up.

4. **The adventure.** Spencer braces the pole with both arms. Savannah climbs Luna's back and reads the knot like a puzzle: this ribbon under, that one over, the blue one all the way around. Thistle ferries ribbon ends. Every time Spencer shifts his grip he jingles, and every crow in three counties comes to look at the shiny prince. Soon there are crows on his shoulders, crows on his head, crows tugging at the spoon. Cora tells them all to get their own.

5. **The twist.** The crows are the answer. Savannah needs the ribbons lifted from above, all at once, and she has no one who flies in a hundred directions. She does now. She unpins Cora's shiny bits from Spencer one at a time and hands each to a crow, and each crow takes a ribbon end in trade, and on her count of three, a hundred crows lift a hundred ribbons straight up and out, and the knot falls open like a flower. Spencer, un-shinied, still holding the pole, cheers so hard he loses his fox-styled hair to the wind.

6. **The resolution.** The festival begins. The ribbons hang straight. Spencer, sweaty and smelling powerfully of mint, does the only sensible thing and wades into the duck pond to wash it off. When he comes out, dripping, hair flat, no bells, Savannah looks at him and says, "There you are." Cora, Finnegan, and Pepper agree, from a distance, that the makeover was a complete success.

7. **Happily ever after.** Ribbons, dancing, Luna wearing the spoon as a medal. Pepper is asked for her cologne recipe by three villagers and is thrilled. Savannah dances with a plain, damp prince, and nobody in the kingdom can tell which of them is happier.
