# Outline: Luna's Approval

**Series:** The Prince Who Kept Showing Up, 3 of 10
**Premise:** Spencer decides that before anything else, he must win Luna's approval, and Luna, grumpy after a windstorm takes down her favorite napping oak, is in no mood to approve of anyone. She sits on him, out-barks him, and steals his snacks. Savannah sees what Luna actually needs, gathers the critters to build a dragon-sized nest, and Spencer earns his approval the only way that counts: by loving Luna every bit as much as Savannah does.
**Characters:** Princess Savannah, Prince Spencer, Luna (center stage), Cora the Crow, Mossy the Turtle, Rascal the Raccoon, Clover the Deer
**Setting:** The great oak at the edge of the castle meadow, where Luna has napped every afternoon of her life, now lying on its side after a night of big wind. The meadow around it, where the nest gets built.

## Beats

1. **The peaceful kingdom.** Prince Spencer had a secret, and the whole kingdom knew it but one. And there was a second one who didn't know, or pretended not to: Luna. Spencer has decided the dragon's approval comes first. This is a problem, because last night's wind blew down Luna's napping oak, and Luna has not napped, and a dragon who has not napped is a dragon with opinions.

2. **The problem.** Luna appoints herself Savannah's guard and will not let Spencer within ten steps. When he comes closer, she barks (the whole kingdom hears it). When he brings her a snack, she takes the snack and the basket. When he sits down to wait her out, she sits on him. Cora the Crow reports every development from the fence post as breaking news.

3. **The call.** Savannah watches Luna pace and grumble and refuse every cushion, and understands: Luna is not guarding anyone. Luna is tired and has nowhere to be tired. "She needs a new spot," Savannah says. "A big one. Today." She sends Rascal to find soft things, Clover to find the sunniest patch of meadow, Mossy to test the ground for lumps (slowly), and Spencer to carry the fallen oak's biggest branches, because nobody else can.

4. **The adventure.** The nest goes up the way everything goes up with these two: Savannah lays out the shape, Spencer hauls the logs, Savannah says "left, no, my left," Spencer moves them. Rascal returns with three blankets, a cushion, and a scarf he swears was given freely. Clover lines the inside with soft moss. Mossy pronounces the ground acceptable. Luna watches all of this from a distance with her back turned, very obviously not interested.

5. **The twist.** Finished, the nest is perfect. Luna will not get in. She sniffs it, circles it, barks at it, and lies down pointedly in the mud instead. Everyone is stumped. Spencer, exhausted, climbs into the nest himself, flops back, and says, "Well, I think it's wonderful." A long pause. Then Luna stands up, walks over, climbs in, and lies down directly on top of him. All of her. He cannot breathe very well. He does not mind at all. "She's sitting on you," Savannah says. "That means she loves you." "I know," Spencer wheezes. "I love her too."

6. **The resolution.** Luna sleeps for the rest of the afternoon. Spencer stays under her the whole time, because moving would be rude. Savannah brings him a lemonade with a very long straw. Cora announces from the fence post that the dragon has given her approval, and repeats it to the whole forest before sundown, adding details.

7. **Happily ever after.** At dusk Luna wakes, yawns a small flame, and moves over exactly enough for Savannah to fit in the nest on the other side. Three of them, one nest, the first stars. Spencer thinks this might be the moment to say it. Luna snores directly into his ear. Not tonight. But close.
