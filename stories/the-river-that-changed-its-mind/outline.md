# Story: The River That Changed Its Mind

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Characters:** Princess Savannah, Prince Spencer, Digby the Mole, Mossy the Turtle, Bramble the Bat
**Reading time:** ~7 minutes

---

## Outline

**The peaceful kingdom:** The little footbridge over the garden river is everyone's favorite spot — ducks paddle under it, Savannah crosses it every morning, and it's never once needed fixing.

**The problem:** Overnight the river changes course. It's pressing hard against one side of the old bridge, and the meadow just past it is starting to puddle. Digby surfaces from his tunnels, soggy and confused — his usual dry paths are flooding.

**The call:** Savannah gathers Digby, Mossy, and Bramble to find out why the river moved before the bridge gives way. Spencer comes too, ready to lend a hand wherever muscle is needed.

**The adventure:** They trace the water upstream. Stacking stones to nudge the current back doesn't hold. Piling branches doesn't hold either. Bramble scouts ahead in the dark and finds the real cause: a family of otters has just moved in upstream and built a new home that nudged the whole river sideways.

**The twist:** The river isn't broken — it's just making room for someone new, and nobody thought to ask the otters what they needed. Savannah realizes the fix isn't to force the water back, but to help it find a path that works for everyone.

**The resolution:** While Spencer braces the old bridge and holds the support stones steady, Savannah and the critters dig a second, shallow channel that lets the river split gently around the otters' new home and still reach the bridge the way it always has. Everyone works together — nobody does it alone.

**Happily ever after:** By evening the water settles into its new, wider path. The bridge is safe, the meadow dries out, and the otters wave from their new home as Savannah and Spencer cross back over together. The kingdom has one more favorite spot than it did that morning.
