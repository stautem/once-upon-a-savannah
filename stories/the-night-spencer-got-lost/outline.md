# Outline: The Night Spencer Got Lost

**Series:** The Prince Who Kept Showing Up, 9 of 10
**Premise:** Spencer finally sets out to say it, by moonlight, carrying a lavender latte, the three-word letter, and every ounce of courage he has. He takes the wrong fork in the fog and gets thoroughly lost. Savannah is out on her own adventure that night, helping the fireflies relight their dark Lantern Tree, and when the tree blazes back to life, its light finds a damp prince standing in the wrong meadow. She is the one who finds him. He does not get to say it. She tells him to tell her in the morning, because it is cold.
**Characters:** Princess Savannah, Prince Spencer, Luna, the Fireflies, Ollie the Opossum, Bramble the Bat
**Setting:** A foggy autumn night. The forked path between Spencer's cottage and the castle. The Lantern Tree, an old hollow beech at the heart of the wood where the fireflies gather every autumn to light it from within, so travelers can find their way.

## Beats

1. **The peaceful kingdom.** Prince Spencer had a secret, and the whole kingdom knew it but one. And tonight, he has decided, is the night. He makes the latte (exactly right). He puts the three-word page in his pocket. He tells Luna, who is staying with him this week, that she cannot come, because this is between him and Savannah. Luna gives him a look that says this is a mistake, and goes back to sleep.

2. **The problem.** Fog. Thick, soft, autumn fog. Spencer reaches the fork in the path where the Lantern Tree's glow should show him the way, and there is no glow. He picks left. Left is wrong. The path narrows, then wanders, then ends at a meadow he has never seen, and the castle is nowhere, and the latte is getting warm.

3. **The call.** Across the wood, Savannah is already out, because the fireflies came to her window in a worried cloud: the Lantern Tree has gone dark. Every autumn they light it from inside, but this year the hollow is stopped up and they cannot get in, and travelers in the fog will have nothing to steer by. Savannah pulls on her boots. Bramble guides her by the paths he knows blind. Ollie comes along and asks, at intervals, whether this is the sort of night where one plays dead.

4. **The adventure.** At the Lantern Tree, Savannah climbs Bramble's directions up to the hollow and finds the trouble: a fat, sleepy hedgehog has moved in for the winter and filled the doorway with leaves. Savannah does not turn him out. She talks to him. She finds him a better hollow, lower down, drier, and Ollie helps carry the leaves. It takes a long, patient, cold hour. The fireflies wait in a nervous glow around her head.

5. **The twist.** The hollow clears. A thousand fireflies pour in, and the Lantern Tree lights from within, gold through every crack in the bark, a lantern the size of a house, throwing light across the whole wood. And there in the light, in the wrong meadow, stands a prince holding a paper cup and looking very lost. Savannah sees him first. She laughs, and calls, "Spencer? What are you doing out here?" He opens his mouth to say the three words. What comes out is: "I brought you a latte." It is lukewarm. She drinks it anyway and says it is exactly right.

6. **The resolution.** They walk home together under the Lantern Tree's light, the fireflies going ahead in a low gold ribbon. He tries twice more. The first time Ollie faints from the excitement. The second time she says, "Whatever it is, tell me in the morning. It's cold, and I've been up a tree." She takes his arm. He decides morning is a fine idea.

7. **Happily ever after.** He walks her to the castle door. She hands him back the empty cup and says, "Thank you for finding me." He says, "You found me." She says, "That's what I said." Luna is waiting on his doorstep when he gets home, awake after all, and she sits on him until he falls asleep.
