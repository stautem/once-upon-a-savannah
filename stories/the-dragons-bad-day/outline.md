# Story: The Dragon's Bad Day

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Characters:** Princess Savannah, Prince Spencer, Luna, Thistle the Rabbit, Hazel the Owl, Rascal the Raccoon

**Reading time:** ~7 minutes

---

## Outline

**The peaceful kingdom:** Luna the dragon always greets the morning the same way — loud, demanding, cuddly, first one awake and first one to say so. It's one of the kingdom's small daily comforts.

**The problem:** One grey, cloudy morning, Luna doesn't. She's quiet. She turns down her favorite treat. She won't play. Nobody can tell why — she isn't hurt, she isn't sick, she's just *off*, and that's somehow more worrying than anything dramatic would be.

**The call:** Savannah decides to figure out what's wrong before the day gets any further. Thistle, Hazel, and Rascal help her investigate. Spencer comes along too, mostly just to keep Luna close and comfortable while the others puzzle it out.

**The adventure:** They try the obvious guesses, one at a time. Maybe she's hungry — they bring her favorite snack; she turns her head away. Maybe she's hurt — they check every scale; nothing. Maybe she's bored — they offer her favorite game; she sighs and closes her eyes. Nothing works, and Luna stays exactly as quiet as before.

**The twist:** While the others keep guessing, Spencer notices something small — Luna keeps turning her head toward the window, toward the flat grey sky, over and over, without seeming to realize she's doing it. He mentions it to Savannah, almost as an afterthought.

**The resolution:** Savannah puts it together: Luna starts every day sunning herself on the tower, and today there's no sun to be had. It was never her stomach or a scrape — she just missed the one small ritual that makes a morning feel like a morning. So Savannah and the others build her a sun of their own — warm blankets, candlelight, everyone curling up close around her on the tower anyway, cloudy sky or not.

**Happily ever after:** Luna perks up, loud and demanding again within minutes, and the kingdom breathes easier. Nobody solved a mystery, exactly — they just noticed what she needed and gave it to her. Spencer gets an extra-loud, extra-grateful nuzzle for spotting it first.
