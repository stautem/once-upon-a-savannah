# Outline: The Map of Favorite Places

**Series:** The Prince Who Kept Showing Up, 8 of 10
**Premise:** Spencer decides the place matters: he will find the one perfect spot to say it. Bramble and Digby help him chart every place Savannah loves, above ground and below, and the map will not stop growing, because Savannah keeps finding new favorites. When Clover the Deer wanders off and does not come home, the map turns out to be exactly the tool Savannah needs, and it leads everyone to a brand-new favorite place, and to the thing Spencer needed to learn: the spot was never the point.
**Characters:** Princess Savannah, Prince Spencer, Luna, Bramble the Bat, Digby the Mole, Clover the Deer, Hazel the Owl
**Setting:** The whole kingdom, drawn in ink: the lavender hill, the pond bench, the footbridge, Wilma's kitchen, the Music Faire square, and a dozen more. A meadow at the far edge of the wood that nobody has mapped yet.

## Beats

1. **The peaceful kingdom.** Prince Spencer had a secret, and the whole kingdom knew it but one. He has the drink, the bench, the song, and three words in his pocket. What he needs now is the right place. So he asks the two who know every path: Bramble, who knows every trail by night, and Digby, who knows every tunnel by touch. They start a map. A star for every place Savannah loves.

2. **The problem.** The map will not stay finished. Every time they ink the last star, Savannah finds a new favorite: a hollow log that echoes, a rock that stays warm after sunset, a puddle that reflects the moon just so. Bramble flies out at night and comes back with three more. Digby adds an entire underground level. The map grows a second sheet, then a third. Spencer cannot choose. There are too many perfect places.

3. **The call.** Clover the Deer, gentle and shy and always there when it matters, has not come home. She wandered out at dawn and it is nearly dusk. Nobody panics; Clover is careful. But the wood is big, and it is getting dark. Savannah says, "Clover goes where I go. She always has. If we know my favorite places, we know hers." Spencer, very quietly, says he might have something that could help.

4. **The adventure.** Savannah unrolls three sheets of map and stares. "Who made this?" "Friends," Spencer says. She does not ask more; there is work to do. They search star by star. The echoing log (empty). The warm rock (empty, still warm). The moon puddle, the lavender hill, the bench. Bramble scouts from above, Digby from below. Hazel flies ahead asking big questions of nobody. At each star Savannah calls Clover's name, softly, the way Clover likes.

5. **The twist.** Clover is at none of them. Then Bramble, circling wide, spots her: past the last star, past the edge of the third sheet, in a meadow nobody has mapped, standing very still and very happy in a sea of lavender that nobody knew was there. She was not lost. She found a new favorite before Savannah did, and stayed to watch the sunset in it. Savannah walks in beside her and says, "Oh." And Spencer, watching her face, understands: the map will never be finished, because she will never stop finding new places to love. The spot was never the point. The point is standing next to her when she says "Oh."

6. **The resolution.** They walk home by Bramble's paths and Digby's shortcuts, Clover in the middle, Luna carrying the map rolled under her wing. Spencer adds one more star, at the very edge of the third sheet, and writes beside it, sideways-spelled: *Clovers Medow.* Savannah fixes the spelling and does not say anything about it. Digby says there is probably a tunnel that goes there. There is now.

7. **Happily ever after.** The map hangs on the wall of the castle kitchen, all three sheets, and is added to whenever anyone finds something worth a star. It has never been finished. Spencer stops looking for the right place. He has decided the right place is wherever she is when he finally opens his mouth.
